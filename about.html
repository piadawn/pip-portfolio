01 / Introduction

An eye for design.
A mind for patterns.

I’m Dawn, a designer and applied mathematics graduate. I work across visual communication and data analysis, turning ideas into images and information into something people can understand and use.

Sofia Dawn Estrada / Philippines

02 / My story

It started with
an obsession.

Films, music, artists, and the urge to make something of my own. I taught myself Photoshop and video editing in high school, making edits and filming school projects.

Years later, leading a creatives committee and contributing to a startup’s rebranding brought that interest into focus. I began thinking beyond how a design looks: who it speaks to, what it communicates, and why each choice belongs.

03 / Creative practice

Give the idea
a visual voice.

I create graphic layouts, brand visuals, and video. My work can be expressive or restrained, depending on what the project needs.

At PUP Mathematics Gradient, I led an 18-member creative team. During my Hustle PH internship, I contributed rebranding concepts, logo explorations, social templates, and marketing materials.

04 / Analytical practice

Find the pattern.
Make it useful.

My background in applied mathematics shapes how I approach a problem: examine assumptions, look for patterns, and test what the evidence supports.

Through consumer research and my undergraduate thesis on online scam dynamics, I’ve worked with survey design, data cleaning, analysis, and mathematical modeling. I bring the same care to communicating the findings through reports, presentations, and visuals.
